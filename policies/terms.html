Sopaan is run by Brain & Mind Academy ("we", "us"). We offer interactive self-paced learning programs and live one-to-one classes in mathematics and the sciences for school and college students, in India and abroad. You can reach us at infosopaanhub@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 96216 90900.
