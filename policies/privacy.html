Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

We collect only what we need to teach your child, send you reports and process your payment. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Who is responsible

Brain & Mind Academy runs Sopaan and is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: infosopaanhub@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

DataWhy we use it
Student's name, class or program, Login ID and PINTo give access to the right program and keep progress together.
Parent's name, email and phone or WhatsApp numberTo send login details, class reminders, progress reports and replies to your questions.
Learning activity: answers, scores, time spent, sheets finished, homework, teacher notesTo show progress to the student, parent and teacher and to plan teaching.
Class schedule, time zone and class linkTo run live classes and send reminders.
Payment details: amount, program, UPI reference or Razorpay payment IDTo confirm payment, enrol the student and keep accounts. Card details are handled only by Razorpay; we never see or store them.
Enquiry details you send usTo reply and help you choose a program.
A device label (for example "Chrome on Android")To keep a login to at most 3 devices.

3. Children

Most Sopaan students are children. We collect a child's data only with a parent's or guardian's agreement, which is given when the parent buys a program, books a class or creates an account for the child. We show no advertising and do not build marketing profiles of children. Teachers see only the students they teach.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with services that help us run Sopaan, and only what they need:

  • Google (Google Workspace, Sheets, Drive and Apps Script) to store accounts, progress and sheets, and Gmail to send emails.
  • Razorpay to process card payments from families outside India.
  • GitHub Pages to host the public website (it stores no student data).
  • Your teacher at Brain & Mind Academy.

We may also share data if the law requires it. We never sell personal data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and progress data while the student is enrolled and for up to 2 years after access ends, so a returning student can continue where they left off. Payment records are kept as long as accounting and tax rules require. You can ask us to delete data sooner (see below), except records we must keep by law.

6. Security

Logins use a personal Login ID and PIN, access is limited by role (student, parent, teacher, admin), and data is stored in our private Google account. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your PIN private and tell us at once if you think someone else has used it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you or your child, stop progress-report emails, or withdraw your agreement for us to use your child's data (which ends access to Sopaan). Email infosopaanhub@gmail.com; we reply within 7 working days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may also contact the data protection authority in your country.

8. Cookies and storage on your device

Sopaan uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember your price currency and save progress on a sheet. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be emailed to account holders.
