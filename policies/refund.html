Refunds, cancellation and delivery

Last updated 26 November 2026

If a self-paced program is not right for you, ask within 7 days of receiving your login and before finishing more than 3 sheets, and we refund you in full. Duplicate or failed payments are always refunded.

1. Delivery

All Sopaan programs are digital and delivered online; nothing is shipped.

How you paidWhen you get access
Card (families outside India, via Razorpay)Automatically, usually within 15 minutes. The Login ID, PIN and link are emailed to you.
UPI (families in India)After our team confirms the payment, usually within a few hours and at most within 24 hours. The Login ID, PIN and link are emailed to you.

If you have not received your login within 24 hours, please check your spam folder, then contact us with your order number.

2. Self-paced programs

  • Full refund if you ask within 7 days of receiving your login and the student has finished no more than 3 sheets of the program.
  • After that, we do not refund self-paced programs, because the full course has been opened to you. If something is not working, tell us and we will fix it or move you to a better-suited program of the same price.
  • One-time purchases (such as sample-paper sets) can be refunded within 7 days if no more than 3 papers have been attempted.
  • If we cannot provide a program you paid for, you get a full refund.

3. Live classes

  • The first demo class is free.
  • Fees for live classes are agreed with you after the demo. Classes already held are not refunded. If you stop, prepaid classes that have not been held are refunded, after taking off any discount that depended on the longer plan.
  • To move a class, tell your teacher at least 12 hours before it starts.

4. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time by emailing or messaging us. Cancelling within the refund window above gives a refund; otherwise access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

5. Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice, or money left your account but the order did not go through, we refund the extra amount in full. Please send us the order number or a screenshot of the payment.

6. How to ask for a refund

Email infosopaanhub@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 96216 90900 with the order number, the student's name and the reason. We reply within 2 working days.

7. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method: UPI refunds to the same UPI account, card refunds to the same card through Razorpay. Once approved, they usually reach you within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank. We refund the amount you paid; we cannot refund fees charged by your own bank or card provider for currency conversion.
